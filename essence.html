Brahma-saṁhitā · 5.1

The Supreme Cause

īśvaraḥ paramaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ
sac-cid-ānanda-vigrahaḥ
anādir ādir govindaḥ
sarva-kāraṇa-kāraṇam

Govinda is the Supreme Lord, whose form is eternal, full of knowledge and bliss. He is the origin of everything and has no origin Himself.

Brahma-saṁhitā · 5.29

Govinda in Goloka

cintāmaṇi-prakara-sadmasu kalpa-vṛkṣa-
lakṣāvṛteṣu surabhīr abhipālayantam
lakṣmī-sahasra-śata-sambhrama-sevyamānaṁ
govindam ādi-puruṣaṁ tam ahaṁ bhajāmi

Brahmā describes the spiritual abode as a realm of transcendental beauty, where Govinda tends the surabhi cows and is lovingly served.

Brahma-saṁhitā · 5.30

The Beauty of Śyāmasundara

veṇuṁ kvaṇantam aravinda-dalāyatākṣam-
barhāvataṁsam asitāmbuda-sundarāṅgam
kandarpa-koṭi-kamanīya-viśeṣa-śobhaṁ
govindam ādi-puruṣaṁ tam ahaṁ bhajāmi

Brahmā glorifies Govinda’s enchanting form: playing the flute, adorned with a peacock feather, and beautiful like a fresh rain cloud.

Brahma-saṁhitā · 5.38

The Eyes of Devotion

premāñjana-cchurita-bhakti-vilocanena
santaḥ sadaiva hṛdayeṣu vilokayanti
yaṁ śyāmasundaram acintya-guṇa-svarūpaṁ
govindam ādi-puruṣaṁ tam ahaṁ bhajāmi

Pure devotees behold Śyāmasundara within their hearts through eyes anointed with the love of devotional service.

Brahma-saṁhitā · 5.40

The Source of Brahman

yasya prabhā prabhavato jagad-aṇḍa-koṭi-
koṭiṣv aśeṣa-vasudhādi vibhūti-bhinnam
tad brahma niṣkalam anantam aśeṣa-bhūtaṁ
govindam ādi-puruṣaṁ tam ahaṁ bhajāmi

The impersonal Brahman effulgence is described as arising from the splendour of Govinda, the primeval Lord.
